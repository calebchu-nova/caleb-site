My story

Why I do this.

01

I'm a VC.

I'm a General Partner at StarWorks. We invest in very early startups building robots, chips and AI hardware. I also started Nova Studio, where I write first and early checks for young founders, often before any big investor shows up.

StarWorks Fund IINova StudioPre-seed & seed
02

But I started as a student founder.

I started my first company at 16, and it failed. Almost nobody around me understood what I was doing.

So I decided to build what I was missing: people who believe in you early, friends who build with you, and a stage you can get on before you succeed. That's why I started Boba Tech, Asia's largest Gen Z founder summit, and why I lead one of Taiwan's largest student startup communities, Stanford ASES Taiwan.

03

What's more: I help international teams connect with Taiwan.

I work with Stanford ASES, a student founder network with members around the world, so I know founders from many countries. Taiwan is one of the best places to build hardware: factories, chips and supply chains. I introduce international founders to that world. And I help young Taiwanese founders go global.
